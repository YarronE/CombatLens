# Observable behavior

- Compatible packages open a readable gameplay interpretation by default. Legacy packages keep their existing overview and action paths.
- A chapter explains a player choice, its design tradeoff, relation to the next chapter, exceptions and explicit evidence gaps.
- Official descriptions preserve source URL, date and scope. Native field facts use physical rows; counts are recomputed from current registered tables.
- Candidate pools remain candidates. Conceptual system relationships never claim unproven native joins, drop probabilities or damage arithmetic.
- Interpretations require their declared current facts. Missing pointers, changed dependencies or invalid records suppress dependent prose, while unrelated chapters remain readable.
- Evidence opens alongside the reading and links to the exact original record. Switching chapters cancels old detail requests.
- Conditional comparison scenarios are analyst questions, with no generated loadout or inferred performance. Build A/B remains a source-checked notebook.
- Hash restoration, keyboard controls and narrow layout preserve reading context.
