# Designer event details
## ADDED Requirements
### Requirement: Evidence-bound explanations
Event details MUST prioritize effect, conditions and related numbers. Every explanation MUST match an explicitly bound rule against original class, action and instance predicates. Analyst parameter edits MUST NOT change evidence eligibility. Missing, ambiguous, stale or unsupported evidence MUST remain unconfirmed, without classname guessing. Skill-stage context MUST remain separate from the selected Notify.
#### Scenario: Scoped rule
A bound original event displays reviewed text and numbers read from exact rule/event paths. Another Buff/Action/required flag or changed source cannot inherit that explanation. Zero and false are retained; absent values are unknown. No damage formula or time unit is invented.
#### Scenario: Freshness
Verify summary hash, Build, its declared dependency hashes and current event-source validity. A malformed/missing guide, stale dependency or changed package yields a compact unknown/error state. Existing research and copy fingerprints remain unchanged by presentation notes.
### Requirement: Event-to-frame location
Selecting a timed event MUST pause and seek the first registered animation sample at/after its mapped start, within the proven selected segment. Show the actual located frame while preserving authored time and units. Raw evidence and analyst timing MUST remain distinguishable.
#### Scenario: Unmapped or late selection
Missing timing, sample sequence, mapping, stale sources or an out-of-segment time cannot fabricate a frame. A late asynchronous selection cannot seek or repaint another event. Scrubbing, event dragging, loop/range controls and history retain their existing timing semantics.
### Requirement: Compact reusable presentation
Default detail shows a short explanation and necessary values. Technical proofs, raw fields and analysis form unfold on demand. Desktop/narrow, graph/source, save/history and no-guide inputs MUST remain usable. Shared renderer and contract MUST serve future generated workspaces; local guides, games and screenshots stay outside Git.
